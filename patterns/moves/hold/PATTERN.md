# moves/hold

## Purpose
The camera does not move; it exists so a story can choose stillness by name.

## Use when / Do not use when
**Use when**
- most beats; a held frame is the default
- you want the lint and the gate to see an intentional stillness

**Do not use when**
- the scene needs life (use idle-breathe)

## Style variables consumed
Reads `ctx.style.motion.travel` (the value of `--mm-travel`) from the composed style; the kit supplies the eased progress with the style's `move` easing. No colour, family, size or duration is read.

## Legibility and rest notes
- Nothing is written, at any p. The layout rests exactly as authored.
