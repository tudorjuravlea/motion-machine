# Pieces

Every folder here is a finished piece: a film or a clicker deck made from one `story.json`, worn in one
`style.json`, and measured by the gate. They are the repository's examples and its regression corpus.
Run the gate on any of them from the repository root:

```bash
node scripts/mm.mjs check pieces/golden-film-editorial
node scripts/mm.mjs serve pieces/golden-film-editorial   # preview in the browser
node scripts/mm.mjs export pieces/golden-film-editorial  # MP4s, poster, loop check
```

`out/` folders (renders, reports, exports) are generated and git-ignored. Faces come from the
shared `styles/fonts/`; `mm new` copies a style's faces into the pieces it scaffolds.

| Family | Pieces | What it is for |
|---|---|---|
| `golden-*` | `golden-film-editorial`, `golden-film-kinetic`, `golden-deck-editorial`, `golden-deck-kinetic` | The reference build of the skill's seven-gate process. One brief (`fixtures/golden/brief.md`) becomes a 30 s film and a ten-press deck, each worn in two styles to show the swap. Each folder keeps its `direction-log.md` (the call at every gate; the crew stood in for the director) and `defects.md` (the ledger of what the gate and the reviews found), the artefacts the skill is meant to produce. `tests/golden/` checks them. |
| `demo-film`, `demo-deck` | one film, one deck | A tour of what the machine makes: its own output shown as pictures (`capture/` renders them and writes both stories from one script). The deck tells the same story press by press. |
| `demo-swap-*` | `demo-swap-editorial`, `demo-swap-kinetic`, `demo-swap-calm-product` | One story in three shipped presets: only `style.json` differs. |
| `demo-launch` | one film | A launch film for a fictional product (Quillmere), built from the launch archetype, with one device scene in seven. |
| `catalogue-*` | 25 films | Every pattern shown, named and explained, then replayed with one knob turned. Generated by `catalogue/build.mjs` from `catalogue/specs/`; see `catalogue/README.md`, which also lists the warnings that stay by design. |
| `demo-hairline-phone` | one film | A phone drawn in thin lines, its page's wire included, that a band turns into the 3D phone before it stands up and its screen scrolls, taps and slides (`3d/phone` move `drawn`). Its fictional app screens are in `sources/` and captured into `assets/`. |
| `sketch-showcase` | one film | The sketch patterns over hand-drawn screens. `build-screens.mjs` renders its screens into `assets/sketch/` and `assets/hifi.png`, which are git-ignored: run it before `mm check`. |

Two things are generated rather than committed:

- the audio of `catalogue-sound-1` and `catalogue-music-1` (`assets/audio/`; see `catalogue/README.md`),
- the screens of `sketch-showcase`.

Everything else passes `mm check` as cloned. Adding a piece under `pieces/` tracks it in git; scaffold scratch
pieces with `mm new --dir <folder>` in a folder you git-ignore.
