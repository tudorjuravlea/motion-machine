Harbour & Pine builds boats that last a lifetime

Every hull is laid by hand in our yard on the estuary, from timber we season ourselves. We take on four commissions a year and no more, so that each one is finished the way it was drawn.

Read about the yard and the people who work in it, or arrange a visit to see a hull on the slip.
