# Golden brief — Motion Machine tells its own story

**For:** the repository README and a launch post. Runs in a feed (muted autoplay) and as a
five-minute talk opener. **Formats:** 9x16, 1x1 and 16x9 for the film; 16x9 for the deck.

**The one thing to remember:** one brief becomes a film and a deck, in any style, and a machine
checks it before you show it.

**Three words for the feeling:** precise, calm, confident.

**Facts (every number on screen cites one):**
- f1 — A 30-second film at 30 frames per second is 900 frames, each drawn from `t` alone. Source: SPEC §2.
- f2 — The gate checks 37 rules: 20 errors and 17 warnings. Source: SPEC §7.
- f3 — The same story renders as a film and as a clicker deck from one scene model. Source: SPEC §1, §10.

**Style:** rendered twice, in `styles/presets/editorial.json` and `styles/presets/kinetic.json`,
to prove the swap. **Faces:** none. **Language:** English, with one Romanian line ("Povești în
mișcare", "stories in motion") to prove Latin Extended glyphs render from the bundled faces.

**Film:** 30 s, eight scenes. **Deck:** ten presses across the same eight scenes.

**Audio:** no track; a declared 120 BPM grid so cues land on beats.
