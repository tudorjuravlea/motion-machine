# Code of conduct

Motion Machine is a small project that depends on people being able to disagree about taste and
evidence without it getting personal. These expectations apply in issues, pull requests,
discussions and any other space where the project is represented.

## What we ask

- Be kind and assume good faith. Critique the work, the measurement or the idea, never the person.
- Be specific and bring evidence. "This rule is wrong" lands better as a piece, a number and a
  suggestion.
- Welcome newcomers. A first pull request deserves a patient review and a clear next step.
- Respect people's time, privacy and boundaries. Do not share anyone's personal details or private
  messages without their consent.
- Credit other people's work. The project borrows ideas openly, and says where from.

## What we do not accept

- Harassment, insults, threats or demeaning remarks, in any form and about any characteristic.
- Sexualised language or attention, and unwelcome contact after someone has asked you to stop.
- Publishing someone else's private information.
- Spam, or using the project's spaces to promote unrelated things.
- Anything else a reasonable person would find hostile in a professional setting.

## When something goes wrong

Report it through GitHub. For a matter that is sensitive, write privately to the repository owner by
the private contact listed on their GitHub profile, or, when the matter also touches security, use
the private vulnerability reporting described in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). For anything that is
not sensitive, open an issue. Reports are read promptly and handled discreetly, and the reporter's
identity is not shared without their consent.

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, close threads, and restrict or remove the participation
of anyone who breaks these expectations. The response is proportionate: usually a private word
first, then a warning, and a ban only for serious or repeated harm. Anyone affected by a decision
can ask for a second look.
